# Routes & Section Architecture

Single-page marketing website for DraftStack.

## Page Map
- Route: `/` (index.html)
- Main Sections:
  - `#top` (Hero): Tagline, CTAs, live GymFlow PH member status dashboard preview
  - `#difference`: "Why DraftStack" comparison table vs typical vendors
  - `#services`: 6 service offering cards
  - `#ai`: AI roadmap section (automation, predictive insights, assistants)
  - `#products`: GymFlow PH product showcase
  - `#about`: Team story ("a small team, building together")
  - `#community`: Facebook group invite
  - Footer: Copyright and social link
